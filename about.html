How We Started

A weekend trip that changed everything

In 2022, our founders struggled to find a genuine, curated stay in Himachal Pradesh — one that felt local, personal, and trustworthy. Every platform showed the same generic listings with unreliable photos and hidden surprises at check-in.

So they built the alternative. WanderStays launched with 12 handpicked properties and a single promise: every listing is verified, every owner is real, and every traveller deserves transparency.

Today we span 18 states and connect thousands of property owners with travellers who care about authentic experiences — from houseboat stays in Kerala to mountain villas in Uttarakhand.

What We Stand For

Our Values

The principles that guide every decision we make

🤝
Trust First

Every property is verified. Every owner is accountable. We don't list what we wouldn't stay in ourselves.

🌿
Local Authenticity

We champion homestays, heritage properties, and owner-run stays that tell the real story of a place.

💬
Transparent Pricing

Travellers can negotiate. Owners control their rates. No hidden platform fees buried in fine print.

🔒
Safe Payments

Payments only flow after confirmed acceptance. Your money is protected until check-in is secured.

⭐
Honest Reviews

Only verified guests who completed a paid stay can leave a review. No fake ratings, ever.

🇮🇳
Made for India

Built around Indian geography, pricing norms, UPI payments, and the way Indians actually travel.
