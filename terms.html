Booking Data

Review Data

Technical Data

Server logs including IP address, browser type, and page access timestamps for security and diagnostics.

🎯 2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the Platform — process registrations, bookings, and payments.
  • To communicate — booking confirmations, status updates, and support responses.
  • To improve the Platform — understanding usage patterns and fixing bugs.
  • To enforce our Terms — detecting fraud or abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law.
We do not use your data for advertising profiling, sell your data to third parties, or share it with advertisers.

🤝 3. Data Sharing

We share limited data with third parties only when necessary:

  • Payment processors (Razorpay/Stripe in production) — receive transaction details but not your full card data.
  • Owners — receive a traveller's name and booking details when a request is sent.
  • Travellers — see an owner's display name and property information.
  • Legal authorities — when required by Indian law or a valid court order.

🔒 4. Security

Passwords are stored using bcrypt hashing — we never store plain-text passwords. Photo uploads are stored with randomised filenames to prevent enumeration. Database connections use parameterised queries to prevent injection attacks.

Despite these measures, no system is perfectly secure. Please use a strong, unique password for your WanderStays account.

Cancellation Policy

Effective: 1 January 2025

⏳ 1. Before Owner Acceptance

If your booking request is still in PENDING status and the owner has not yet responded, you may simply allow it to lapse. No payment has been taken at this stage, so no cancellation action is required.

If your request is REJECTED, you may revise your offer once or choose not to proceed — again, no charge applies.

✅ 2. After Acceptance — Before Payment

Once a request is accepted, you have until the owner's stated deadline (if any) to complete payment. If you decide not to proceed:

  • Contact the property owner directly via the contact details on the property page.
  • Since no payment has been made, no refund is applicable.
  • Repeated non-payment after acceptance may result in account restrictions.

💳 3. After Payment

Once payment is completed and the booking is confirmed, cancellation terms are set individually by each property owner. Three standard tiers are commonly used:

✓
Flexible
Full refund if cancelled 48+ hours before check-in
◑
Moderate
50% refund if cancelled 5+ days before check-in
✗
Strict
No refund within 7 days of check-in
⚠ Always check the specific cancellation policy on the property page before completing payment. The policy is set by the owner and WanderStays is not liable for refund disputes.

🏠 4. Owner-Initiated Cancellations

If an owner cancels a confirmed, paid booking, the traveller is entitled to a full refund of the amount paid. Repeated owner cancellations may result in listing suspension or removal from the Platform.

💰 5. Refund Process

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on the bank or UPI provider. WanderStays facilitates refund requests but is not responsible for delays caused by financial institutions.

To initiate a refund claim, email support@wanderstays.in with your booking reference.
