How WanderStays works

Whether you're looking for the perfect stay or want to list your property, the process is clear, fair, and fully transparent.

1

Create your free account

Sign up with your name, email and password. Select "Traveller" as your role. No credit card required to register — you only pay when you book.

💡 Your account stores your full booking history and preferences.
2

Explore destinations

Browse destinations across 15+ Indian states. Filter by state, property type (hotel, resort, villa, Airbnb, homestay), and sort by minimum nightly price to find what fits your budget.

💡 Each destination card shows the starting price so you can compare before clicking in.
3

Pick a property

Click a destination to see all available properties. Browse photos, read descriptions, check amenities and guest reviews. Sort by price to find the best value.

4

Send a booking request

Choose your check-in and check-out dates, set your number of guests, and enter your offered price per night. You can bargain below the listed price — the owner decides whether to accept.

💡 All values are validated — no negative numbers, no past dates, guest count capped at property maximum.
5

Wait for the owner's response

Your request status will show as PENDING. The owner can accept, reject, or set an acceptance deadline. Track everything from your My Trips dashboard.

If REJECTED, you get one more chance to revise your price or cancel. If ACCEPTED, you move to payment.

6

Pay securely

Pay using your credit/debit card or UPI. Payment is processed only after the owner has accepted. The total is calculated based on your agreed nightly price × number of nights.

7

Stay & review

Enjoy your stay! After payment is confirmed, you can leave a star rating and written review directly on the property page. Only guests who actually paid can review — keeping ratings honest.

💡 Reviews help other travellers and reward great owners with higher visibility.
1

Register as an owner

Create an account and select "Property Owner" as your role. Your dashboard is separate from travellers and designed around managing listings and requests.

2

Add your destination

Before listing a property, add the destination it belongs to — choose the state, primary property type, write a description, and upload up to 8 photos (drag & drop supported).

💡 Destinations are shared across the platform, so your location helps other owners in the same area too.
3

List your property

Add your property linked to a destination. Set your nightly price, max guests, amenities (comma separated), a description, and upload property photos. Everything saves to the database immediately.

4

Receive booking requests

Your dashboard shows all incoming requests with the traveller's name, dates, guest count, and their offered price. If the offer is below your listed price, it's flagged clearly.

5

Accept or Reject with a deadline

Click Accept to confirm the booking. You can optionally set a deadline by which the traveller must complete payment. Click Reject to decline — the traveller can then revise their offer once.

💡 Setting a deadline keeps bookings from going stale. The traveller sees it on their request status.
6

Track payments and reviews

Your dashboard shows a PAID badge once the traveller completes payment, and tracks your total revenue. Guest reviews appear on your property cards so future travellers can see your reputation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us →

Can I bargain below the listed price?▾
Yes — when you enter your offered price, you can go below the owner's listed rate. A warning will appear noting that the offer is below listed price. The owner then decides whether to accept, and can reject if the price is too low. You get one revision opportunity after a rejection.
What happens if the owner sets a deadline?▾
If the owner sets an "accept by" deadline, you'll see it on your My Trips dashboard and on the property page. The deadline is informational — it's the owner's way of communicating urgency. Make sure to complete payment before then.
Is my payment information stored?▾
No. WanderStays does not store card numbers or CVVs. In production, payment processing would be handled by a PCI-compliant gateway such as Razorpay or Stripe. The current version records only the method used (card or UPI) for display purposes.
Can I cancel a booking?▾
If your request is still pending, you can let it lapse. If it has been accepted but not yet paid, contact the owner directly. Cancellation and refund policies are set individually by each property owner.
How do reviews work?▾
Only travellers who completed a paid booking can leave a review on that property. This prevents fake or incentivised reviews. You can leave one review per booking. Reviews are visible to all travellers on the property page.
Can an owner list multiple properties?▾
Yes — there is no limit. An owner can add multiple destinations and list multiple properties under each. All listings appear on the owner's dashboard with individual booking histories and revenue tracking.
